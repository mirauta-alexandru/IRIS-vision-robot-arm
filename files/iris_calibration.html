Exported drafts always have enabled=false and calibration_verified=false. These two points only define a provisional linear mapping. Do not treat them as mechanical travel limits unless you have separately measured and checked both safe endpoints with the linkage and power disconnected as appropriate.

These jog windows are commissioning limits, not final servo travel calibration. CH1 uses 1550 µs as its center reference; the other channels use 1500 µs. All final angle/pulse limits remain unset and all channels remain disabled for normal motion.
